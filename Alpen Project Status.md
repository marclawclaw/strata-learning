# Alpen Project Status

As of **2026-11-04**:

- **Network**: Alpen **Testnet III** on the default bitcoin **signet**. It went live **2026-07-28** and is the "partner integration phase", described as the final major validation stage before mainnet.
- **Mainnet**: **no announced date.** An earlier target of mainnet in 2025 (from the 2024 $8.5M strategic round) slipped.
- **Partners**: 21+ integrated, including bridge operators Stakely and Chainflow, with P2P.org expected (press and aggregator sources).
- **Regenesis**: the previous testnet (chain ID 8150) was decommissioned on 2026-08-18. The current chain ID is 20310 ([[Alpen Testnet Network Details]]).
- **Bridge**: [[Glock]]-based. Press describes it as the first publicly usable garbled-circuit bitcoin verifier.
- **Architecture**: Alpen and the bridge are reportedly decoupled and independently upgradeable, with no regenesis needed.
- **Starknet**: a Starknet Foundation grant funds a **shared Glock verifier** as a public good.

**Timeline:**
- 2024: "Strata" announced as a BitVM2-based bridge rollup ([[Strata Bridge BitVM2-era Design]]); $8.5M strategic round led by DBA and Cyber Fun ([[Alpen Funding]]).
- 2025-07-15: Glock teaser post.
- 2025-08-19: Glock paper (2025/1485) and repos released.
- 2025-08 → 12: audits ([[Alpen Audits]]).
- 2026-04: [[Mosaic]] paper (2026/812).
- 2026-07-28: Testnet III.
- 2026-08-18: old testnet (chain 8150) decommissioned after a regenesis.
